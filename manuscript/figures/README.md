# Figures

Storyboard, sources and conventions for the manuscript and supplement figures. Every figure is generated by
`manuscript/scripts/make_figures.py`; `MANIFEST.json` records the SHA-256 of each input and output and the
selection rules used. PDFs are the publication files (vector, raster only inside heatmaps and dense scatters);
PNGs are previews for review.

## Visual conventions

Defined once in `manuscript/scripts/figstyle.py`.

| Role | Colour | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Measured data, exact quantities | near-black | dose matrices, certified roots, reference-only predictors |
| Held-out-pair benchmark models | orange `#E69F00` | F0/F2 networks, GP, CF, DeepSynergy, ridge |
| Same-pair predictors | sky blue / blue / bluish green | B1 pair offset / B2 cross-cell linear / B3 pair-seen network |
| Historical pipeline | vermillion `#D55E00` | SynergyFinder 2.0.12 reconstruction (solver branch matching 2.0.3) and summed-dose fallback, native scores |
| Correction | blue `#0072B2` | certified roots, continuous extension, C1 restoration |
| Controls | reddish purple `#CC79A7` | permutations, donor pairs, deranged anchors, simulated zero interaction |
| Uncertainty and noise | grey | replicate noise, noise ceilings, replicate resampling |

Inhibition uses one sequential map (cividis, −10 to 100%); interaction residuals and contrasts use one
diverging map (RdBu_r, symmetric about 0). Figure text is Computer Modern 5.5–8 pt at final size (cmr10, cmb10 for panel letters, mathtext for symbols), matching the manuscript; figures are 6.3 in wide and placed at that size. `figstyle.save` refuses a figure whose text uses a character cmr10 cannot print outside math, or whose drawn content runs past its edge.
Intervals are 95%: thick bars or shaded bands in the series colour are pair-cluster bootstrap intervals, thin bars
delete-one-drug jackknife intervals. Noise, ceilings and replicate resampling are grey.

## Case-selection rules

- **Figure 1 and S1.** Among the 22,422 pair-cells measured in one batch, the pair-cells whose Bliss pair-cell
  contrast $t_{pc}$ is closest to the 5th, 50th and 95th percentiles of all 22,737 values (ties: first in pair,
  cell order). The rule was fixed before the cases were viewed.
- **Figure 6a.** The Figure 1 case whose native Loewe surface uses the fallback (the 95th-percentile case; the other
  two cases have no fallback units).
- **Figure 7.** Dasatinib and MK-2206 in MSTO (native surface 9458), chosen for illustration, not at random. It
  is one of 568 surfaces with co-increasing LL.4 arms, a weaker plateau below 100 and at least four grid cases
  each of accepted roots, rejected certified roots and certified no-root cases. Population diagnostics are in Figure S5 and use the stated certificate subsets.
